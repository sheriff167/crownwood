The yard

Everything that leaves this yard was a log when it came in.

01 / THE LOG

It starts
at the mill.

Every board we sell is sawn in our own yard in Nakuru. No middlemen between the log and your site.

02 / THE CUT

You name
the size.

The saw opens the log into slabs, then boards. Tell us the size and length you need and we set the saw to it.

03 / THE BOARD

By the piece
or the lorry.

Rafters, purlins, battens, boards and shuttering for your roof, floor or site. Small orders welcome. Big ones too.

04 / THE DOOR

Then we
build it.

When you need more than timber, the workshop takes over: doors, windows, frames and furniture made from wood we cut ourselves.
