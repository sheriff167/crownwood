Ordering

How to order

Most orders are sorted in one WhatsApp chat or phone call.

Send your list

Sizes, lengths and quantities, e.g. “4×2 × 14ft, 30 pieces, cypress”. A photo of your fundi’s list is fine.

Get a price

We confirm the price, what is in stock, and how long any cutting will take.

Collect or deliver

Pick it up at the yard on Mawanga Road, or we load it and deliver to your site.
